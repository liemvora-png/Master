## Findings

1. **R-02 (HIGH) - Em dash in text.** `—` appears in the `<title>` of all 7 templates (line 17 each, e.g. `Liem Vora — Portfolio`), in `public/assets/portfolio-admin.js:221` (`${booking.name} — ${booking.lesson_date}...`), and in seeded `metaTitle` values in `migrations/0001_site_content.sql` (lines 44, 49, 54) and `migrations/0004_services_gallery_bookings.sql` (lines 59, 62). DB meta titles flow into `document.title` at runtime. Replace with comma, colon, or pipe.

2. **R-25 (HIGH) - Muted text fails WCAG AA.** `--muted: #73766a` on `--paper: #f4f1e9` measures **4.11:1** (needs 4.5:1 for normal text) and **3.45:1** on `--paper-deep: #e7ddcd`. Muted is used for intro copy, project descriptions, footer text, empty states, and editor help. Dark mode passes (6.22:1 / 5.80:1). Fix: darken the light-mode `--muted` token to about `#62655a` or darker (target at least 4.5:1 on both paper surfaces).

3. **R-03 (HIGH) - Tap targets below 44px on mobile.** At ≤700px, `.theme-toggle` and `.admin-entry-button` shrink to `min-height: 36px`; `.site-nav a` links are ~0.68rem text with no padding (~16px tall). All interactive mobile targets must be ≥44px.

4. **R-03 (HIGH) - h1 overflows at 320px.** `h1 { font-size: clamp(3.4rem, 8.4vw, 6.8rem); letter-spacing: -0.055em }` with no `overflow-wrap`. Measured with the real Playfair Display latin subset at 54.4px (the clamp floor at 320px): "Accomplishments" = **392px**, "arrangements." = **318px**, against ~282px available width. Long single-word page titles overflow the viewport. Fix: lower the clamp floor (e.g. `clamp(2.4rem, 8.4vw, 6.8rem)`) and/or add `overflow-wrap: break-word` to headings.

5. **R-03 (HIGH) - ~9px text at ≤420px.** `.header-note, .project-link` drop to `font-size: 0.58rem` (~9.3px) in the ≤420px media query. Unreadably small; keep at least 0.75rem.

6. **R-26 (HIGH) - Dead-link fallback `href="#"`.** In `public/assets/site.js`, `updateNavigation` and `renderProjects` fall back to `href="#"` when a DB href/URL is missing or invalid, producing clickable links that go nowhere. Omit the link (render plain text) or skip the item instead.
