South Korea launches "Hangang," a 614.7-petaflop supercomputer built on ~8,500 Nvidia GPUs

South Korea's sixth national supercomputer, "Hangang," was formally launched Monday at KISTI in Daejeon after a 382.5 billion won ($276M) build. With roughly 8,500 Nvidia GH200 Grace Hopper superchips, 205 petabytes of storage and 614.7 petaflops of theoretical peak performance, it delivers 24x the compute of its 2018 predecessor Nurion and enters full service in December, with plans to link it to a quantum computer in 2027.

"Weight-Space Programming": GPT-6 Astra edits another model's weights from plain-English instructions

In Monday's blog post "Sparks of Weight-Space Programming," researchers associated with MIT CSAIL report that OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra can read the raw weights of another neural network and edit them directly from natural-language instructions, with no gradient descent or retraining. Editing just 0.016% of Alibaba's Qwen3.5-0.8B weights raised its MATH-500 score from 7.9% to 48.1%. The work is early-stage and not yet peer-reviewed.
